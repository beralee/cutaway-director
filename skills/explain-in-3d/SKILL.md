# Direct a 3D explainer

Start with a question the viewer should be able to answer. Pick one thing to follow, one camera route and one moment to stop and inspect the evidence. Follow the user's chosen language, format, duration and budget. Defaults are Chinese narration, 16:9 and 24 fps. A single question typically fits 45–90 seconds.

1. **Research and storyboard.** Extract necessary facts and their source locations. For a YouTube source, read timestamped captions with `scripts/read_youtube.py` and check the video ID. Distinguish narration, visible evidence and your interpretation. Give each shot a starting state, a visible change and an observable result, then add narration, sound cues, timing and asset IDs. See [production notes](references/production.md).
2. **Choose how to produce the image.** Build accurate geometry, mechanisms and controlled comparisons in one continuous Three.js scene. Animate the parts and camera with GSAP. For realistic environments, use consistent subject and location references and specify one camera action per generated shot. Combine the routes when useful. Mark illustrative reconstructions and changes of scale. See [four directing patterns](references/astra-method.md).
3. **Prove the hardest ten seconds.** Use the proposed scene or footage. Include the move, the stop and the evidence. Watch it muted. Then check that the key action coincides with the words that explain it. Record accepted assets, actual external spend and remaining budget, including retries. If the user requested approval of the proof, deliver it and wait. If commissioned to finish the film, check the proof and continue. Choose local production for a zero external-generation budget.
4. **Build and layer the film.** Freeze the action when a relationship needs explanation. Outline or label it on the same frame, then resume. Keep footage, overlay, narration, music and sound effects individually editable. Time effects to actions and leave room for speech. Follow the installed HyperFrames CLI's contract. See [the working scene starter](references/astra-method.md#render-the-included-starter). The local fallback accepts a computed renderer or per-shot clips with `scripts/compose.py`. On Windows, `scripts/speak-zh.ps1` supports local Chinese speech. Disclose substitutions when the user specifies an engine.
5. **Revise the affected layer.** Adjust camera easing for a rough move, replace an overlay for a label change, and replace the relevant stem for a sound change. Regenerate footage when the content of that footage needs changing. Retain the editable project and update time and cost records.
6. **Check and deliver.** Review transition boundaries, the start and end of holds, labels, captions and the identity of the followed object. Decode the complete MP4 and verify duration, frame size, frame rate and audio. Deliver the requested movie or storyboard, relevant sources and any illustrative assumptions. State which routes were actually run.
